1. BACKGROUND AND INCORPORATION 
This Data Processing Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between:
Medalverse Co., Ltd. (the "Company" or "Data Processor"), and the legal entity, educational institution, or individual registering for an account or utilizing the Platform (the "Organizer").
This Agreement supplements and forms an integral part of the existing commercial contract, Master Services Agreement, or Terms of Service (the "Principal Agreement") executed between the Company and the Organizer. This Agreement legally establishes the rights, liabilities, and obligations of both Parties regarding the processing of personally identifiable information under the Personal Data Protection Act of Thailand (PDPA) and other applicable data protection laws. In the event of any conflict between the provisions of the Principal Agreement and this Agreement concerning data privacy, the terms of this Agreement shall absolutely prevail.
2. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION
Data Processor: Means the technology provider processing personal data strictly on behalf of, and under the instruction of, the Data Controller.
Data Subject: Means the identified or identifiable natural person attending an event or claiming a certificate, specifically including students and event participants.
Organizer: Means the corporate partner, educational institution, or event host utilizing the platform, acting exclusively in the capacity of the Data Controller.
Personal Data: Means any information relating to a Data Subject, explicitly including names, academic records, email addresses, and photographic or biometric media uploaded to the platform.
Services: Means the educational technology system, artificial intelligence profile generation tools, and digital certificate aggregation platform provided by the Company.
3. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY
3.1. Processing Parameters. This Agreement strictly governs the processing of Personal Data by the Company. The Company shall process Personal Data solely to execute the Services and shall act exclusively upon the documented technical and operational instructions provided by the Organizer.
3.2. Precedence of Terms. This Agreement constitutes a legally binding addendum to any master service agreement, general terms of service, or commercial contract executed between the Company and the Organizer. In the event of any conflict regarding data privacy obligations, the protective clauses established within this Agreement shall absolutely supersede the general commercial terms.
3.3. Photographic Media and Event Documentation. The processing parameters of this Agreement explicitly govern any photographic media, event recordings, or biometric data uploaded to the technology infrastructure of the Company by the Organizer. The Organizer assumes absolute legal responsibility for verifying the lawful basis for processing any media containing the facial identity of a Data Subject prior to initiating an upload.
4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTIES
4.1. Designation of Roles. The Parties expressly acknowledge and agree that for the purposes of applicable data protection laws, the Organizer operates exclusively as the Data Controller and the Company operates exclusively as the Data Processor.
4.2. Processing Instructions. The Company shall process the personally identifiable information of the Data Subject solely in accordance with the documented instructions of the Organizer. The Company is explicitly prohibited from processing such data for any independent purposes outside the scope of the Services.
5. OBLIGATIONS OF THE DATA CONTROLLER
5.1. Lawful Basis and Warranties. The Organizer represents and warrants that it possesses a valid lawful basis to collect, process, and transfer the personal data of the Data Subject, specifically including student records, to the Company.
5.2. Statutory Consent. The Organizer assumes absolute and strict liability for obtaining explicit, informed, and documented statutory consent from the Data Subject prior to utilizing the platform and transmitting any data to the Company.
5.3. Photographic Media and Consent. The Organizer assumes absolute liability for the capture and upload of photographic media or video recordings containing the identifiable likeness of any event participant. The Organizer must secure explicit statutory consent or provide legally sufficient crowd notices at the venue prior to uploading such media to the technology infrastructure of the Company.
5.4. Indemnification. The Organizer shall fully indemnify, defend, and hold the Company harmless against any regulatory fines, civil claims, or damages arising directly or indirectly from the failure of the Organizer to secure necessary statutory consents, or from any unauthorized data transfers initiated by the Organizer.
6. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND ALGORITHMIC PROCESSING
6.1. Algorithmic Processing Authorization. The Organizer hereby grants the Company formal and explicit authorization to utilize proprietary and third-party generative algorithms on the provided data. The Company shall utilize these artificial intelligence services strictly for the purpose of executing platform features, specifically including the synthesis of educational records and the generation of professional resumes for the Data Subject.
6.2. Limitation of Generative Liability. The Company provides all algorithmic outputs and generated content strictly on an "as-is" basis. The Company explicitly disclaims any liability or warranty regarding the factual accuracy, completeness, or professional viability of the generated resumes. The Organizer and the Data Subject bear the absolute responsibility for reviewing, editing, and verifying the generated outputs prior to any academic or professional utilization.
6.3. Prohibition on Model Training. The Company expressly prohibits the utilization of the personally identifiable information of the Data Subject for the purpose of training, fine-tuning, or enhancing any foundational machine learning models. The Company guarantees that personal data processed through external application programming interfaces shall not be retained by third parties for algorithmic training purposes.
7. SUB-PROCESSING AND THIRD-PARTY INTEGRATION
7.1. General Sub-Processing Authorization. By executing the commercial agreement, the Organizer grants the Company blanket authorization to engage third-party sub-processors necessary for the delivery of the Services. This specifically includes the engagement of external cloud infrastructure providers and artificial intelligence application programming interfaces.
7.2. Sub-Processor Obligations. The Company shall ensure that all engaged sub-processors are bound by strict, written contractual agreements. The Company warrants that these agreements shall impose data protection standards and confidentiality obligations identical to, or no less protective than, those established within this Agreement and mandated by the Personal Data Protection Act of Thailand.
7.3. Right to Object. The Company shall provide the Organizer with written notification prior to the addition or replacement of any critical sub-processor. The Organizer retains the right to object to such changes within fourteen days of the notification, provided that the objection is firmly rooted in reasonable, documented data protection or security concerns.
8. CROSS-BORDER DATA TRANSFERS
8.1. International Data Flow Compliance. In the event the provision of the Services requires the transfer of personally identifiable information outside the jurisdiction of Thailand, the Company shall ensure such transfers are executed using compliant mechanisms. The Company shall maintain data routing practices that align with the adequacy requirements of the Personal Data Protection Act of Thailand and the General Data Protection Regulation.
8.2. Standard Contractual Clauses. Where required by the jurisdictional domicile of a foreign institutional Organizer, or where data must be routed to a jurisdiction lacking an adequacy decision, the Parties agree to execute the requisite standard contractual clauses or equivalent legal transfer instruments to guarantee the lawful international processing of the data.
9. DATA SECURITY AND INCIDENT RESPONSE
9.1. Technical and Organizational Measures. The Company shall implement and maintain comprehensive technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the Personal Data against unauthorized access, accidental loss, alteration, or unlawful destruction. These measures shall strictly align with industry best practices for cloud infrastructure and artificial intelligence processing environments.
9.2. Data Breach Notification. In the event the Company becomes aware of a confirmed security incident resulting in the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure of the Personal Data provided by the Organizer (a "Data Breach"), the Company shall notify the Organizer without undue delay, and in no event later than twenty-four hours after verifying the Data Breach.
9.3. Remediation and Cooperation. Following a Data Breach, the Company shall promptly take all necessary and advisable corrective actions to mitigate the effects of the Data Breach. The Company shall provide the Organizer with all reasonable cooperation and technical information required for the Organizer to fulfill its statutory reporting obligations under applicable data protection laws.
9.4. Personnel Confidentiality. The Company shall ensure that all internal employees, contractors, and authorized personnel who are granted access to the Personal Data have committed themselves to strict obligations of confidentiality, or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality. Access to Personal Data shall be strictly limited to personnel who require such access to perform the Services.
10. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS ASSISTANCE
10.1. Technical Assistance. The Company shall provide the Organizer with the necessary technical functionality and operational support to enable the Organizer to fulfill its obligations to respond to requests from a Data Subject exercising their rights regarding access, rectification, or erasure under applicable data protection laws.
10.2. Direct Communication Prohibition. The Company shall not respond directly to any request received from a Data Subject concerning their Personal Data. The Company shall promptly forward any such inquiries directly to the Organizer and shall only act upon the written instruction of the Organizer regarding the resolution of the request.
11. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND DATA OWNERSHIP
11.1. Personal Data Ownership. The Parties acknowledge and agree that the Data Subject retains absolute legal ownership of all original raw Personal Data submitted to the platform.
11.2. Institutional Ownership. The Organizer retains absolute legal ownership of all institutional trademarks, logos, and digital certificates issued through the platform.
11.3. Platform and Output Ownership. The Company retains exclusive and absolute intellectual property rights over the platform architecture, software source code, visual design templates, and the proprietary methodologies utilized by the Artificial Intelligence Services.
12. DATA RETENTION, RETURN, AND DESTRUCTION
12.1. Processing Duration. The Company shall process and retain the Personal Data solely for the duration of the active Principal Agreement between the Company and the Organizer, or until the Organizer formally requests deletion via the platform interface.
12.2. Post-Termination Obligations. Upon the expiration or termination of the Principal Agreement, the Company shall, at the written direction of the Organizer, either return a complete copy of the Personal Data to the Organizer or securely and permanently destroy all Personal Data stored within the systems of the Company.
12.3. Exemption for Claimed Output. Notwithstanding the data destruction obligations, the Company shall not be required to locate, recall, or destroy digital certificates or algorithmic resumes that have already been transmitted to, downloaded by, or independently stored by the Data Subject prior to the termination of the Principal Agreement.
13. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
13.1. Infrastructure Exemptions. The Company shall not be held liable for any data loss, breach, or service interruption arising directly from the failure, negligence, or downtime of external cloud infrastructure providers or third-party artificial intelligence application programming interfaces.
13.2. Financial Cap. The total aggregate liability of the Company arising out of or related to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall be strictly limited to the total fees actually paid by the Organizer to the Company during the twelve months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the liability.
